Essay: College Culture & Substance Use

I plan to write about the differences in student's actions once they're away from their parents, and how social media, pop culture, and society can influence it. After graduating from high school ive seen more and more people talk about drinking, partying, and doing different substances and I was curious on why that is.

In high school kids would drink, smoke, party, and do a lot things they shouldn't be doing. But in college it feels like people embrace it and are somewhat normalizing it even though there's a higher likelihood of things going wrong. In college you have a higher risk of getting laced and now there's RA's watching you trying to catch you using drugs or drinking. And if you get caught, there's a chance you get kicked out.

To me it feels like students do this because they want to celebrate the freedom from their parents, think of it as the college experience, or maybe feel pressured or inclined to do it because others around them are doing the same thing. I also think movies, shows, and social media have also influenced how people see college and these activities, in movies and shows like Grown-ish and Animal House college is shown as a place where you party and experiment with yourself, and over social media you can find posts from people who talk about their experiences with it. Not all of it is bad, parties can be ways to unwind and make new friends but at some of these parties people are doing drugs and drinking, things that can potentially lead to having a lot of issues in the future. And i think they're taking it from the parties and starting or continuing to do it in their day to day lives.

XY Thesis: Media may be normalizing college students doing harmful substances, which could lead to students influencing other students to trying things.
